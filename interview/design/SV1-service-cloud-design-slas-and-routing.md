# SV1 · Service Cloud design: SLAs and routing

**Level:** Medium · **Time:** 25 min

## Brief

Premium customers get 1-hour first response and 8-hour resolution; standard customers 8 hours and 3 days. Cases come by email, web chat and phone; route to agents by language and product skill.

## Answer

- **Entitlements** on accounts (Premium, Standard) with **entitlement processes**: milestones First Response and Resolution, with warning and violation actions (notify, escalate).
- Email-to-Case, Messaging for In-App and Web, and voice channels create cases with origin and language.
- **Omni-Channel skills-based routing**: skills for language and product; Omni-Channel flow sets required skills; capacity per agent.
- Agentforce service agent on web chat for deflection, escalating to Omni-Channel with context.
- Reports and dashboards on milestone compliance.

## Talk track

Entitlements make SLAs measurable; skills-based routing matches work to the right agent; agents deflect routine requests.
